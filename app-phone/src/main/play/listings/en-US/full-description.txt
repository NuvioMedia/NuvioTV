Omnio brings the Stremio addon ecosystem to your phone with a polished, touch-first experience.

Sign in once and your addons, plugins, profiles, watch progress, and library follow you across devices. Includes a built-in player powered by ExoPlayer and mpv, with subtitle, audio, and stream selection.

Features:
- Sign in with email or scan a QR from Omnio TV to instantly sync your account.
- Browse catalogs from any Stremio-compatible addon.
- Local playback with hardware-accelerated decoders (FFmpeg, AV1, IAMF, MPEG-H).
- Picture-in-picture, lock-screen controls, and Bluetooth headset support.
- Multi-profile with optional PIN lock per profile.
- Cross-device sync via a personal Supabase backend.

Omnio for phone shares the same account and feature set as Omnio TV, so your library is always one tap away.
